Privacy Policy

What data Harfika processes, and how ads and the purchase work.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short. Harfika has no server and needs no account. Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored on your phone and is never sent to us. If Google backup is turned on, Android may also include it in your own Google account backup (section 1).

The game is free and supported by ads. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which collects some device information for advertising (see section 3).

The “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Data stored on your phone

All of the following is stored in the app's data on your phone. Other apps cannot access it, and it is not sent to us:

DataWhat it is and why
Game progressYour current level, the words you found, the hints you used
Coins and bonusYour coin balance, a log of coin transactions (so a reward is never given twice), bonus words waiting to be collected
Daily rewardThe day of your last reward and your streak
StatisticsIn-game counters such as words found and your longest word
SettingsWord language, sound, vibration and reduce-motion preferences
Purchase statusWhether you bought “Remove ads” (Google Play verifies this on each launch)

What we do not collect: name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools. We do not sell your information.

If you email us (see Contact) we will of course see your address and message. We use them only to reply to you.

Android backup: If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the game data above in the encrypted backup in your Google account, so your progress comes back when you switch phones or reinstall the app. Android and your Google account manage this backup; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off or delete it in your phone's Google backup settings.

2. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and verify purchases. The game itself can be played offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): for AdMob ads (section 3).
  • Vibration: for in-game haptic feedback; can be turned off in Settings.
  • Google Play billing: for the “Remove ads” purchase.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect from your device: the advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, ad interactions and approximate location derived from the IP address. This information goes to Google, not to us, and is processed under Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent on first launch using Google's consent form. If you do not consent to personalised ads, non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Ads between levels are never shown in the first levels and only occasionally after that. Rewarded ads (e.g. “watch an ad, +50 coins”) open only when you choose them.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Your card or payment details never reach us. The app only learns from Google Play whether the purchase was made and stores that on your phone. The purchase is tied to your Google account; on a new phone, “Restore purchase” brings it back. Details: Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Children

Harfika is designed for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ad content is limited to a teen-appropriate rating. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data on your phone. “Clear storage” in Android settings does the same. If your Google account backup holds a copy, you can delete it in your phone's Google backup settings.
  • For data collected by AdMob, you can use Google's tools (My Ad Center).

We hold no copy of your game data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. If you want the emails you sent us deleted, just write to us.

7. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR give you the right to access, correct, delete and port your data. Because your game data lives on your phone (and, if enabled, in your own Google backup), you exercise these rights directly. For data processed by AdMob, you can contact Google.

8. Changes to this policy

This text may be updated when the app gains new features. The current version is always on this page; the date above shows the latest change.
