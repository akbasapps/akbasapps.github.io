About our apps

Rutinim tracks daily routines and habits, with reminders, a timer, streaks, statistics and home screen widgets. Privacy policy

Ayla is a period and cycle tracker with predictions, daily logs, symptoms and insights. It does not even hold the internet permission, and your records are stored encrypted on your phone. Privacy policy

Harfika is a word puzzle game that combines a letter wheel with a crossword: 600 levels in English and Turkish, and a new corner of the world every 20 levels. It is free with ads, which can be removed with a one-time purchase. Privacy policy

No account, no server: your data stays on your phone. Rutinim and Ayla have no ads.
