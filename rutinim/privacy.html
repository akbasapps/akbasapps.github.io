Privacy Policy

What data Rutinim handles, where it is kept, and what it never does.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

In short. Rutinim has no server. Your routines, records and notes are stored on your phone. Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it.

There are two exceptions: Drive backup uploads a file to your own Google account, and buying Premium goes through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account to create.

1. What data we handle

Rutinim only handles the data it needs to work. You create all of it, and all of it stays on your phone:

DataContents and purpose
RoutinesName, icon, color, goal, unit, duration, reminder times, days and notification sound
Progress recordsWhich routine you did on which day, and how much
Check-in timesThe time of day you checked off a routine. Used by the "What time" chart
Timer sessionsDate, start time and length of sessions you ran with the timer. Used by the time statistics
Day notesA short note you write for a day
Deleted routinesThe history of routines you deleted, so they still appear in statistics. Deleting one permanently removes it completely
App settingsTheme, language, notification preferences, badges, the running timer, and whether Premium is unlocked

Data we do not collect: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, location, contacts, photos, device identifier, advertising ID, IP address logs, usage statistics, crash reports.

If you email us (see Contact), we will of course see your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to reply to you.

2. Where your data is kept

On your phone

All of your data is stored in the app's private storage. On Android, other apps cannot access this storage. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Google Drive (only if you choose)

If you connect your Google account in Settings and tap Back up now, or turn on Back up automatically, a copy of your data is uploaded as a file to your own Google account. The file stays in your Drive; we cannot access it, see it or keep a copy of it.

The app asks Google only for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can see only the files it created itself. Your other documents, photos and folders in Drive stay closed to the app. You can delete backup files from Drive yourself at any time.

Files you save yourself

With Export data you can save a backup to a location you choose. That file is entirely your responsibility; it contains your routine names and history unencrypted.

Android's own backup

Android offers automatic backup at the system level, and Rutinim leaves it on. This is what moves your data when you switch phones. That backup is kept in your Google account and is subject to Google's own privacy terms; you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

3. Buying Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the entire payment:

  • Only Google sees your card and payment details. We do not see or store them.
  • To unlock Premium, the app only asks Google Play whether this account has bought Premium, and keeps the answer on your phone.
  • So that we can keep sales records, Google reports order details to us (such as the order number, product, date, price and country). Your name, email address and payment details are not part of these reports.

Your purchase record stays in your Google Play account. How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Rutinim does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or advertising ID permissions.

5. Sharing

Rutinim does not share, sell or rent your data to anyone. There is no data exchange with ad networks, analytics providers, data brokers or any other third party.

The only outside services the app talks to belong to Google:

  • Google Drive: when you back up. The data goes to your account.
  • Google Play: when you buy Premium, and when the app checks at startup whether Premium is unlocked. Nothing about your routines is sent to Google Play.

6. What appears on your screen

Widgets show routine names on your home screen, and notifications show reminder text. You can choose what appears on the lock screen in your phone's notification settings.

7. Children's privacy

Rutinim is not directed at children. Because it collects no age, identity or contact information, it does not process personal data belonging to children.

8. Deleting your data

All of your data is under your control:

  • One by one: you can delete routines and records inside the app. A deleted routine can also be deleted permanently from the Statistics screen.
  • Everything: uninstalling the app deletes all data on your phone. "Clear storage" in Android settings does the same.
  • Drive backups: delete them yourself at drive.google.com.
  • Drive connection: you can remove Rutinim's access at any time on the third-party connections page of your Google account.

Since we keep no copy, there is no need to send us a deletion request; there is nothing for us to delete. If you want emails you sent us deleted, just ask.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security; other apps cannot reach the app's private storage. Uploads to Drive and communication with Google Play use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Keep in mind that an exported backup file is not encrypted. Anyone you share it with can read it.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app gains new features. The current version is always on this page, and Settings → Privacy policy in the app links here. The date above shows the latest change. If an update changes how data is handled (for example, adding ads), we will also announce it inside the app.
